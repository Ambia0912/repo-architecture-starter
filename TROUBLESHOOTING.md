# Troubleshooting & Incident Post-Mortems

Record real production incidents here. Every entry must follow the 5-part format.

---

### Incident Template
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Symptom**: What broke or threw an error?
- **Root Cause**: Why did it fail?
- **Fix**: What was changed?
- **Automated Guard Added**: Which test was created so this class of bug cannot happen again?
